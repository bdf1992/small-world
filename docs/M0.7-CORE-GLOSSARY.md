# M0.7 Core Glossary

Status: design candidate from owner glossary review; owner acceptance pending.

This document narrows the active M0.7 language before more implementation or interface work. It intentionally defines only the smallest terms needed to reason about Artifact collapse, elemental interaction, state, topology, traversal, and presentation.

It extends the reset in `M0.7-MINIMAL-ARTIFACT-GRAMMAR.md`; it does not reintroduce the named semantic fields removed by that reset.

## Element

One of the eight fixed elemental positions:

```text
Void → Fire → Chaos → Ground → Aether → Water → Order → Sky → Void
```

Elements are primitive positions in a cyclic relation system, not independent authored stat fields.

## Identity

The Element occupying the `Is` / origin seat for an Artifact's elemental reading.

Identity orients the canonical elemental profile. It is analogous to choosing the phase/origin of the same cyclic relation shape rather than authoring a new profile for every Element.

Example when Identity is `Fire`:

```text
Fire   = Is
Chaos  = Affinity
Ground = Anchor
Aether = Vice
Water  = Nemesis
Order  = Conflict
Sky    = Need
Void   = Wants
```

Changing Identity rotates the same canonical relation pattern around the eight-element ring.

## Profile

The canonical directed relationship pattern around an Identity.

The profile defines relative elemental roles such as `Is`, `Affinity`, `Anchor`, `Vice`, `Nemesis`, `Conflict`, `Need`, and `Wants` by clockwise/counter-clockwise offset from the Identity seat.

A Profile is therefore derived from:

```text
canonical elemental relation shape
+ Identity orientation
= elemental Profile
```

Properties and current conditions may vary how that profile is applied without redefining the global meaning of an Element.

## Attribute

A weighted elemental relation expressed through an Artifact's Profile.

Attributes are the elemental character available to interactions. They are where the eight Elements, their relative roles, and their weights become usable by Clock, Hourglass, Grain, Artifact-to-Artifact, and other elemental interactions.

An Attribute is not a generic semantic field such as age, mobility, moisture, race, or material.

## Property

A rule or configuration that changes how Attributes or other admitted relationships behave, resolve, constrain, or propagate.

Properties can modify elemental weights, alter how a crossing is interpreted, gate behavior by Clock/Hourglass context, or otherwise vary an Artifact's operation without redefining the canonical elemental ring.

Properties are not constrained to being downstream of Stats. A Stat or Statline may satisfy a condition that results in a Property becoming active, and a Property may in turn affect Attributes or later measurements.

## Stat / Statline

A measured value or collection of measured values that participates in resolution.

Stats are not required to be terminal outputs. A Statline may be read by rules and relations and may contribute to later Properties, Attributes, crossings, or state changes.

The model is therefore not a one-way hierarchy such as:

```text
Attribute → Property → Stat
```

It is a bounded stateful relation graph in which typed measurements and rules may affect one another across admitted resolution steps.

## State

The measured record of what is true at a particular time/resolution point `T`.

State is evidence, not the rule that changes the world.

Conceptually:

```text
State(T)
  ↓ read by admitted rules / relations / Properties
proposed effects
  ↓ constraints + propagation + collapse
State(T+1)
```

State may record resolved Identity, active Attributes, active Properties, Statline, relations, positions, Clock/Hourglass condition, and other measured facts. This is not a frozen storage schema.

## Address

A place, state, boundary, or object the system can uniquely refer to.

An Address need not be geometric. A Clock tick, Hourglass side, POI, zone boundary, threshold, or Artifact may all be addressable.

## Position

Where an entity currently occupies an addressable structure.

Position is measured as part of State.

## Relation

A declared or derived typed, weighted, and admissible connection or potential connection between addressable things.

Relations may be derived from relative structure, points, boundaries, zones, Attributes, State, constraints, and available resolution budget.

A Relation does not have to be a fully materialized Artifact.

Example:

```text
POI.A ── weighted/admitted relation ── POI.B
```

With additional resolution budget, the same connection may become explicit enough to materialize as an `Artifact.Road`. The Road may then carry its own Identity, Attributes, Properties, Stats, State, and relations.

Artifactization is one possible deeper resolution of a Relation, not the source of the Relation's existence.

## Traversal

Actual movement through an admitted Relation from one addressable position toward another.

Traversal is not teleportation. An entity must move through the structure connecting its prior and next addressable positions unless a future explicit mechanic defines a different traversal rule.

## Crossing

A witnessed passage during Traversal through a meaningful boundary into a newly addressable state or position.

Examples already native to the design shape include:

```text
Clock Hand crosses Tick
Grain crosses Hourglass Neck
Persona crosses Zone boundary
measured value crosses Threshold
```

A state delta must not masquerade as a Crossing unless an admitted traversal through the relevant boundary occurred.

Crossings are causal resolution points: they give rules, Properties, relations, constraints, and propagation an opportunity to react before the next measured State settles.

## Artifact

An addressable thing in the world/system.

Artifacts may be unresolved, partially resolved, or fully realized. Artifact type describes what an Artifact may become; it does not imply a matching Card.

## Virtual

A valid unresolved or partially resolved possibility.

Virtual state remains first-class. Lack of resolution budget does not make a possibility invalid.

## Instance

A sufficiently collapsed realization of a Virtual possibility at an addressable collapse position.

## Collapse

Budgeted resolution that makes Virtual possibilities more specific while preserving unresolved frontier when budget or constraints stop further resolution.

Collapse may resolve type, topology, elemental identity, Tokens, relations, composition, or other admitted state without requiring every dimension to settle at once.

## Budget

The explicit limit on how much resolution may occur.

Existing dimensions include:

```text
maxHops      → relation depth
maxSlots     → opened composition edges
maxInstances → Virtual → Instance materializations
```

Budget changes resolution depth, not authored meaning or validity.

## Card.Artifact

The active generator of Artifact possibilities.

```text
Card.Artifact
    ↓
Virtual<Artifact>
    ↓ weighted / constrained collapse
Artifact.<Type>
    ↓
Instance<Artifact.<Type>>
```

There is no requirement for `Card.Persona`, `Card.Item`, `Card.Biome`, or matching Cards for every Artifact type.

## Pack

A composition of possibilities, Artifacts, and relations used to author or constrain a local resolution context.

Pack remains a composition mechanism, not a parallel runtime.

## Token

A weighted semantic candidate available during collapse.

Tokens may help resolve, refine, name, or configure an Artifact. A Token is not itself an Attribute, Property, Stat, or State.

Detailed Token vocabulary and eligibility rules remain deliberately open.

## Sprite

A generative visual projection of resolved Artifact state.

A Sprite may be selected, composed, recolored, have parts or overlays changed, or otherwise be procedurally adjusted from Identity, Attributes, Tokens, Properties, State, and presentation context.

Sprite output is presentation. It does not become authoritative simulation state merely because it visually represents that state.

## Core invariants

1. **One elemental shape, rotated by Identity.** Do not author eight unrelated elemental profiles where one canonical cyclic relation function suffices.
2. **Attributes are elemental character.** Do not reintroduce arbitrary semantic root fields under `Attribute`.
3. **Properties change behavior.** They may vary, constrain, gate, or interpret Attributes and other admitted relationships.
4. **Stats participate.** Stat/Statline may be both cause and consequence in bounded resolution; it is not forced to be a terminal scalar output.
5. **State is the measured record.** State describes what is true at `T`; admitted rules and relations explain change toward `T+1`.
6. **Crossings require traversal.** Do not silently teleport an entity between addressable states and call the delta a Crossing.
7. **Relations may remain derived.** A connection does not require an Artifact merely to exist; deeper resolution may materialize one when useful.
8. **Budget reduces resolution, not validity.** Virtual frontier remains valid first-class state.
9. **Presentation is downstream.** Generative Sprite variation may reflect resolved state but must not silently become world authority.
10. **Keep the why.** Resolved State should remain inspectable back through the relations, crossings, Properties, measurements, constraints, and collapse decisions that produced it.

## Deliberately soft seams

The following remain intentionally unresolved until implementation proves they need stronger contracts:

- concrete Token vocabulary and Token Pack semantics;
- exact thresholds for when a derived Relation should materialize as an Artifact such as a Road;
- the complete rule language for Property / Statline / Attribute propagation;
- whether every traversable relation must first expose an explicit intermediate path address;
- detailed Sprite composition and fallback grammar;
- type-specific semantic content.

Do not fill these seams with speculative fields merely to make examples look complete.
