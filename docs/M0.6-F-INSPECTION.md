# M0.6-F — Plain Inspection Views

## Purpose

Keep the solver sophisticated while giving developers and designers simple handles over its state.

The inspection surface is intentionally four concepts:

```text
Facts         what exists
Possibilities what may still become true
Lineage       how this value reached its current stage
Signals       what the solver resolved, propagated, or stopped
```

## Facts

Facts are emitted for Instances only. They summarize identity, grammar, properties, attributes, rarity, exact stats/state, ownership/location, member relationships, and remaining component slots.

## Possibilities

Possibilities are emitted for Virtuals only. They expose fixed authored facts, weighted possibility fields, ranges, and unresolved slots without pretending those values have settled.

## Lineage

Lineage shows the plain lifecycle chain, normally:

```text
Definition → Template → Reference → Virtual → Instance
```

## Signals

Signals are a developer-facing summary of a solve trace, not raw kernel objects. They expose:

- budget usage;
- typed stop reasons;
- node resolved/unresolved state;
- stage/grammar/id of resolved values;
- possibility/range/slot keys for Virtuals;
- fact families for Instances.

## World graph view

A whole realized world can be projected into one inspectable graph view containing:

- Regions, extents, boundaries, backing Biome Artifacts, and slots;
- Situations, Region addresses, Pack forms, and member Artifacts.

This is a semantic/runtime view, not a rendered map. UI geometry may be added independently.
